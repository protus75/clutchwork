Write a post around one question the selected audience cares about. Pick a real incident, design decision, or worked example from the supplied material. Let the requested Tone facet shape the voice. Use descriptive headings suited to this story and follow the order below.

## Opening

Begin at the moment the problem becomes visible. Give the reader one concrete observation and enough context to understand its consequence. Use a date, quotation, number, or first-person account only when the source supports it. A hypothetical example must be identifiable as such.

## What happened and why

Walk through the evidence in the order that makes the discovery understandable. Identify the decision, who or what made it, and the constraint that shaped it. Explain a technical term when it first affects the story. Use a short excerpt or command only when its exact text comes from the owning material and the reader needs it to follow the result.

## Outcome and takeaway

Show the observable result and the conditions under which it held. For a Clutch run, ground claims about a merge, rejection, or retry in the supplied run artifacts. If the story concerns a draft design, end with the proposed outcome and the test that would demonstrate it. Preserve uncertainty about cause and scope. End with the lesson the evidence warrants or the question that remains.

On derivation, keep the source sections used available to the enclosing surface. Shorten by selecting a narrower story or omitting audience-irrelevant detail where fidelity permits. Check anecdotes, causal language, and headline claims against the canonical source before returning the post.
