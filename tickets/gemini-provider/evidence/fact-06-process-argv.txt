A0 fact 06: process-argv
gemini-cli 0.49.0, captured 2026-11-15 (UTC 2026-11-16).

FINDING
Every call runs as TWO node processes: the launcher and a relaunched child that adds
--max-old-space-size=<n> (source: RELAUNCH in bundle/gemini.js). Both carry the full user argv.
- npm global install under nvm (this machine's `gemini`): argv is
  `node <nvm>/bin/gemini <args>` and child `<nvm>/bin/node --max-old-space-size=20054 <nvm>/bin/gemini <args>`.
  The string "@google/gemini-cli" does NOT appear in the argv (the bin is a symlink to bundle/gemini.js).
- pnpm global install (pnpm 10.34.5, isolated PNPM_HOME in scratch): the shim execs
  `node <PNPM_HOME>/global/5/.pnpm/@google+gemini-cli@0.49.0/node_modules/@google/gemini-cli/bundle/gemini.js <args>`,
  and the child is `<abs node> --max-old-space-size=20054 <same path> <args>`.
A live_call matcher on "node .../@google/gemini-cli/..." works for pnpm and misses npm/nvm installs;
matching either argv[1] ending in "/bin/gemini" or containing "@google/gemini-cli/" covers both, and
the relaunch pair should be labelled once (the child is the one doing the work).
