A0 fact 14: list-models
gemini-cli 0.49.0, captured 2026-11-15 (UTC 2026-11-16).

FINDING
The CLI has no model-listing subcommand or flag (gemini --help lists mcp, extensions, skills, hooks,
gemma only; /model is an interactive dialog). Its built-in VALID_GEMINI_MODELS table is partial
(gemini-3-pro-preview, gemini-3.1-pro-preview[-customtools], gemini-3-flash-preview, gemini-2.5-pro,
gemini-2.5-flash, gemini-3.5-flash, gemini-3-flash, gemini-3.1-flash-lite, two gemma-4 ids) and
omits every image model. The API's ListModels endpoint (GET
generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/models, key in the x-goog-api-key header) listed 64 models
for this key, but it is not a reliable "served" probe: gemini-2.5-pro is listed yet a call returns
404 "no longer available to new users" (fact 02). Owner-note names gemini-3-pro and gemini-3-flash
are not served ids (gemini-3.1-pro-preview and gemini-3-flash-preview are); the three image models
gemini-2.5-flash-image, gemini-3-pro-image-preview and gemini-3.1-flash-image-preview are listed.
So GeminiAdapter.list_models has no CLI source: options are ListModels over HTTP (API-key auth only,
not OAuth) filtered to generateContent models, or a static list, each confirmed by a probe call.
